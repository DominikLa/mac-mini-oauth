Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

mac-mini is a personal tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google account. It accesses Gmail and Google Calendar data solely so that the owner's own AI assistant, running on the owner's own computer, can read that mail and calendar and prepare drafts on request.

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's encrypted computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: the developer contact address listed on the app's consent screen.
